How this review schedule works

New words are due now. Good advances through 1, 3, 7, 14, and 30 days, then stays at 30 days. Again resets the word and brings it back in 10 minutes. Intervals run from the time you rate the word.

This is a simple fixed schedule, not a personalized or validated prediction of memory. Undo restores the previous schedule unless a newer review on another device has changed that word.
